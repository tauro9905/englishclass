01 / THE UNIT

Start with who
you are.

This unit helps A1 students learn basic English while exploring their community, cultural identity, and local traditions. Students will learn to describe their community, share traditions, and learn about other cultures.

The unit promotes inclusion, respect, participation, and intercultural understanding.

Topic Identity and Territory
Subtopic My Community and Traditions

What will you learn?

By the end of the unit, you will be able to:

  1. Identify basic vocabulary related to communities, families, places, and traditions.
  2. Describe simple aspects of your community using basic English sentences.
  3. Talk about local traditions and cultural practices.
  4. Identify similarities and differences between your culture and other cultures.
  5. Show respect for different cultural identities and perspectives.
